Core Capabilities

Why CivicShield AI?

Responsible AI

🛡️ Decision Support, Not Autonomous Action

CivicShield AI provides decision-support intelligence for human emergency responders. AI outputs are recommendations only and must be verified by authorized human personnel before any emergency action is taken. CivicShield AI does not autonomously dispatch police, fire, or medical services, and does not replace emergency communication systems. Always call your local emergency number (e.g., 112 / 911) in a life-threatening situation.
